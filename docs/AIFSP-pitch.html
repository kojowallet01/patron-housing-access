ACCRA INSTITUTE FACULTY STUDENT PORTAL CAMPUS ACCESS & ATTENDANCE SYSTEM
Proposal To the Institute Administrator

A system that lets every campus gate verify who belongs on campus in seconds and keeps a single, time-stamped record of who is on site — live, across all five campuses, from one place.

The problem

  • Gates admit people on trust and memory — there is no fast way to confirm a student or visitor truly belongs.
  • There is no reliable record of who is on campus at any given time, or when they arrived and left.
  • Each campus operates on its own — the institute has no single picture of activity across its locations.

What the system does

Students register once (name + phone) and carry a personal code. At the entrance, security scans the code and gets an immediate, verified answer: registered student of this campus — or not. Every entry is logged automatically with a timestamp. No notebooks, no guesswork.

What it gives the institute

Live
Who is on campus now

Trusted record & audit trail
Five
Campuses, one standard
  • Safety first. In an emergency, the institute knows exactly who is on each campus — instantly.
  • Accountability. Verified attendance and entry logs that settle any dispute without argument.
  • Gate control. Unauthorized people are identified and recorded at the door, not after an incident.
  • Central view, local control. One system serves Tesano, Cantonment, Ashiaman, Legon and Tema — leadership sees everything, each campus manages only its own.
  • Professional image. A modern, managed front door reflects an institution that takes order and safety seriously.

The ask

Adopt this as the institute’s official access system. It is already live and working today — I can demonstrate it in two minutes. My proposal: run it officially at one campus for a week, review the daily reports, then roll out to the rest.

Cost is minimal: hosting only (~$25/month, less on a yearly plan). No new hardware — students and gate staff already have phones. I will train the security teams and provide simple one-page guides.
